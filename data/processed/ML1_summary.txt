## Missing Data Strategy

Unmatched population values were retained as missing values.

Missing population was NOT replaced with zero because
an unmatched Census ZCTA does not imply zero population.

## Important Limitations

1. NPPES was sampled rather than processed in its entirety.
2. The sample should not be interpreted as the complete US provider network.
3. ZIP codes and Census ZCTAs are not identical geographic concepts.
4. Some NPPES records contain special/territory/military-style addresses.
5. Provider density contains extreme values in low-population areas.
6. Provider count does not directly represent provider capacity,
   appointment availability, quality, or actual access.
7. Final access-gap target definition is owned by ML-2/other project members.
